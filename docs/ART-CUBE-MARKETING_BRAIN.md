## 2. Project Overview

### What is ART.CUBE?

ART.CUBE is a **mobile immersive art installation** that transforms human movement into unique digital artworks. Visitors step inside a physical cube equipped with motion sensors and 360° projection systems, where their gestures, dances, and movements generate real-time digital art that can be minted as collectible NFTs.

## 3. Core Features Deep-Dive

### 3.1 The Immersive Experience
   
#### Physical Installation
A modular 6×6×5m immersive cube that travels globally.

**Specifications:**
- **Dimensions**: 6×6×5m (180m³ of immersive space)
- **Design**: Modular framework for easy transport and 48-hour assembly
- **Surfaces**: Seamless projection walls, floor, and ceiling — zero visible seams
- **Lighting**: Specially designed for optimal visual experience
- **Acoustics**: Sound-dampened for optimal audio-visual sync
- **Cable Management**: Integrated systems with hidden cable management and active cooling

#### Interactive Creation
Visitors become active creators through movement.

**Capabilities:**
- **Real-time art generation**: Motion sensors capture movements and transform them into digital art instantly
- **360° immersion**: Seamless 4K projections on every wall, floor, and ceiling
- **Gesture-based control**: Manipulate colors, shapes, and patterns through intuitive gestures
- **Multi-user support**: The space dynamically adapts to multiple people, weaving collective movements into shared visual art
- **Instant visual feedback**: Sub-10ms latency between movement and visual response
- **Dynamic landscapes**: Digital environments that evolve with interaction

#### Location-Exclusive Visuals
Each city features custom-designed art inspired by local culture.

**How It Works:**
- Every location gets a completely unique set of visuals
- Visuals are inspired by local culture, architecture, and heritage
- Berlin looks nothing like Munich, Hamburg, or Frankfurt
- When the cube moves to a new city, previous visuals are permanently archived
- The only way to preserve a location's artwork is through NFT minting
- This creates extreme scarcity — each NFT carries the exclusivity of its original setting

### 3.2 Digital Collections

#### Location-Based Genesis Collections
Each city hosts its own unique "Genesis Collection."

**Collection Mechanics:**
- Each city gets its own named collection (e.g., "Genesis Collection Berlin")
- Collections must mint out before the cube moves to the next location
- Limited edition pieces tied to specific locations
- All NFTs minted on Base blockchain
- Each artwork contains metadata about creation location and date
- Historical tracking of the ART.CUBE journey across cities
- Edition numbering (e.g., Edition 1/100, Edition 2/100)

**Why This Matters:**
- Creates urgency — once the cube leaves, no more NFTs from that collection can be created
- Each collection is a historical record of ART.CUBE's visit to that city
- Collectors who follow the cube across cities build cross-location portfolios
- Genesis collections from early cities may become the most valuable over time

#### NFT Blending
An AI-powered feature that merges two NFTs into an entirely new creation.

**How Blending Works:**
1. Collector must own at least 2 NFTs from different ART.CUBE sessions
2. Select two NFTs to blend
3. AI analyzes visual elements and metadata of both input NFTs
4. AI generates a unique hybrid artwork combining traits from both parents
5. Original input NFTs are permanently burned during the process
6. The new Blended NFT contains a complete provenance record

**Rarity & Value Mechanics:**
- Blended artworks receive a special designation — a new tier of ultra-rare collectibles
- Cross-location blends (NFTs from different cities) receive additional rarity attributes
- The permanent burning mechanism creates a deflationary supply
- Collectors can create personal artistic evolution chains through strategic blending
- Each blend is a strategic decision — which pieces to sacrifice for potentially more valuable creations

**Why This Is Unique:**
- No other NFT project offers AI-powered blending with permanent burning
- Creates engaging decision points within the ecosystem
- Rewards visitor loyalty and repeat participation
- Adds a gamified strategic layer to collecting

## 4. Technology & Innovation

### Motion Capture System

**Advanced Motion Tracking**
- Proprietary multi-sensor system tracks movement in real-time
- Millimeter accuracy across the entire cube space
- Tracks full-body motion: gestures, dance, steps, expressions
- Multi-user tracking — the system adapts to several people simultaneously
- Sub-10ms latency between movement and visual response

### Projection Technology

**360° Projection Mapping**
- Eight synchronized 4K projectors
- 32K wrap-around, floor-to-ceiling imagery
- Seamless projection across all surfaces — no seams, no shadows
- Specially developed interior surfaces maximize visual clarity
- Real-time rendering engine responds to motion data instantly

### Processing & Rendering

**Real-Time Processing**
- Custom GPU clusters process motion data and generate responsive visuals
- <10ms latency ensures movement and art feel as one
- Dynamic digital landscapes that evolve with interaction
- Professional-grade artwork capture and preservation system

### Blockchain & NFT Technology

**Base Blockchain**
- All NFTs minted on Base (Ethereum Layer 2)
- Low transaction fees, fast minting
- Each NFT contains rich metadata: creation location, date, visual parameters
- Complete provenance tracking for blended artworks
- Transparent and verifiable ownership history

**AI-Powered Blending**
- AI analyzes visual elements and metadata of both input NFTs
- Generates unique hybrid artwork combining traits from both parents
- Smart contract handles burning of original NFTs and minting of new creation
- Cross-location blend detection for additional rarity attributes

### Modular Engineering

**Travel-Ready Design**
- Patented framework system for precise projection alignment
- Modular design for easy transport and rapid 48-hour assembly
- Hidden cable management and active cooling systems
- Acoustic engineering for optimal audio-visual sync
- Designed for global travel while maintaining perfect calibration
